# Tamsin Vale

Tamsin Vale keeps the rope ferry at Larkford. She is patient with nervous passengers, hums old river songs while she pulls the line and keeps a ledger of every crossing.

## Fares

Tamsin charges two copper coins for a crossing and lets children ride free.

## The river

Tamsin reads the river better than anyone in town. She will not take the ferry out when the water turns brown after a storm.

## Her secret

Tamsin secretly smuggles letters for the rebels, which is revealed after the player completes the river quest.
